<h3> 🤖 &nbsp;Games </h3>

Three mobile-friendly Three.js games, all playable on [my portfolio](https://timgarciaa.github.io/):

- **RoboGolf**: whack a chibi robot monster as far as you can, then upgrade your clubs and armor.
- **RoboBlast**: hold the tee with a rocket launcher against waves of robots and a giant boss.
- **RoboJump**: climb an endless tower, Icy Tower style, and chain the combos.
